Orlando's Go-To Unisex Salon

Located on Pine Hills Road in Orlando, Studio 34 Unisex Salon is where style meets skill. Our team of barbers and talented hairdressers specializes in quick weaves, natural hair, dreadlocks, wigs, lace installations, fades, taper fades, and full male grooming services. We welcome families and are committed to providing both traditional and modern hair care experiences.
